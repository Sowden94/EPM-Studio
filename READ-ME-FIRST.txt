This update requires the existing complete EPM-Studio repository. The scientific datasets and original dependencies are unchanged and are not included again.

Changes:
- Nearest visible surface is blended once, avoiding front/back triangle transparency artefacts.
- Software fallback interpolates smooth vertex colours with a depth buffer instead of painting flat translucent triangles.
- Higher-detail atoms, bonds and label textures.
- Native 3840 x 2160 PNG stills with readable method and scale captions.
- Full HD/QHD/4K WebM movies encoded at a deterministic 30 fps, with a complete turn.
- HD 1280 x 720 GIFs at 20 fps with a fixed palette to reduce colour flicker. GIF is limited to 256 colours; PNG/WebM give smoother full-colour output.
- Export framing includes the surface envelope and prevents zoomed-in cropping.

Movie export uses native WebCodecs VP8/VP9 support, available in compatible browsers such as current Chrome/Edge. If an encoder is unavailable, the app explains this; no recognition runtime, server or additional third-party library is introduced. Rendering may take longer than the movie's playback duration.

Validation:
- Browser: smooth surfaces visually checked for formaldehyde and a newly drawn water molecule using the software fallback.
- Browser: PNG decoded at 3840 x 2160.
- Browser: VP9 WebM decoded at 1920 x 1080, exactly 4 seconds, with 120 encoded frames.
- Browser: GIF decoded at 1280 x 720, 80 frames at 50 ms with a fixed 243-colour palette.
- npm test passed, including nearest-depth/duplicate-transparency regression checks and a real VP8 WebM bitstream checked with ffprobe.
- WebGL hardware rendering and 4K movie encoding were not exercised in this test browser; they remain dependent on device/browser support.
